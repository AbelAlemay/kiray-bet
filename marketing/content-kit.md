### C. Safety tip 1: never pay first
```
⚠️ የደህንነት ምክር: ቤቱን በአካል ሳያዩና ውል ሳይፈርሙ ምንም ገንዘብ አይላኩ።

Safety tip: never send a deposit or rent by Zelle, Cash App or any app before you've seen the home and signed a lease. Anyone who asks you to pay first is likely a scammer.
KirayBet never asks for money. 👉 kiraybet.org/safety
```


## 3. Answers to common questions (for comments and DMs)

- **Is it really free?** "Yes, 100% free to search, message and post. ነጻ ነው።"
- **Is it safe?** "Every listing is reviewed before it goes live, you chat privately without sharing your phone number, and anyone suspicious can be reported. Never send money before seeing the home. ደህንነትዎ ቅድሚያ ነው።"
- **How do I post my place?** "Go to kiraybet.org, tap 'Post a home', sign in with your email (no password), add photos and details. We review it and it goes live, usually within a day."
- **Which cities?** "Denver, DC/Maryland/Virginia, Seattle, Dallas–Fort Worth, Austin, Houston, Minneapolis–Saint Paul, Portland, Atlanta, the SF Bay Area and Southern California."
- **Is it only for Habesha?** "KirayBet is built for our community, in Amharic and English, but every listing is open to everyone by law (Equal Housing Opportunity)."
- **There's nothing in my area yet.** "We just launched! Turn on post notifications or join t.me/kiraybetus, and tell anyone renting out a room that they can post free."

## 4. Weekly routine (about 30 minutes)

1. Check the admin page: approve new listings, answer reports.
2. Post 2–3 times (plan above) and one story.
3. Share one post in 2–3 community groups (ask admins first).
4. Message 2 people who might rent out a room or basement.
5. Look at Search Console → Performance once a week to see what people search.
